# Requirements

The app follows two sources:

1. **Content and look:** [`Before_It_Happens_Journal.pdf`](Before_It_Happens_Journal.pdf), *Before It Happens: Alex's Journal*
   (32 entries, November 2026). All kids' and grown-ups' wording in the app comes from this file.
2. **How the app behaves:** Part A of [`Before_It_Happens_Kids_Book_v1.md`](Before_It_Happens_Kids_Book_v1.md), the original
   app brief. Its rules still apply: offline and private, no ads or accounts, tap-to-dial only, PIN for personal pages,
   read-aloud, large tap targets, TalkBack labels, and a 'Who can help' button on every kids' screen.

## What changed from the first edition (22 stories)

| Area | First edition | Alex's journal (current) |
|---|---|---|
| Narrator | Third person | Alex (Year 5) writes each entry as a journal page |
| Stories | 22 stories, 3 parts | 32 entries with a day, in 5 sections: Friends and fitting in (1–8), Tricky choices (9–17), Phones, games and group chats (18–23), Big feelings (24–29), Keeping safe (30–32) |
| Entry layout | What's happening?, Remember, Say this | Journal paragraph, comic, What I figured out, What I can say right now, Later (26 of 32 entries), Magic Words, Try it!, Grown-up Corner |
| Core idea | Say it out loud | **Exit now** with a low-key line, **tell quietly later**; some things you always tell straight away |
| Crew | Zara, Sam, Kai, Ruby, Theo, Mia | Alex joins; new descriptions; Mum, Dad, Ms P, a canteen worker, Alex's little brother and others appear |
| Games | Surprise or Tell? (11) | **Keep It or Tell It?** (12, with "the test") |
| Pages | — | New **My Exit Lines** page (pick favourites, write your own, grown-ups to tell quietly later) |
| What If? | 27 cards | 27 new cards, each linked to an entry |
| Good or Bossy Friend? | 7 + 7 | 8 + 8 |
| Tech Rules | 12 | 14 (two new rules about sharing and mean group chats) |
| Look | Bright picture book | Lined notebook paper, handwriting font (Patrick Hand), line-drawn comics with pastel colour |

## Decisions made when building it

- **My Turn** (a private text box on each entry) isn't in the journal PDF but is kept from the app brief, using the brief's prompt.
- **Skin tones:** the PDF's line drawings leave faces uncoloured. The app gives each character a light skin tone so the cast stays visibly diverse.
- **Similar-looking grown-ups:** the PDF draws Dad, the "grown-up" in Entry 11, the great-uncle and the stranger at the park with the same picture. The app gives each a different top colour so the stranger never looks like Dad.
- **Progress reset:** read ticks, favourites, collected Magic Words and My Turn answers belonged to the old story numbers, so they start fresh after the update. Names, Safety Hand, scores, plans and the PIN are kept.
- **Section colours** are darker versions of the PDF's pastel caption colours, so white text on the home tiles is readable. Comic caption bars use the pastel shades, as in the PDF.
